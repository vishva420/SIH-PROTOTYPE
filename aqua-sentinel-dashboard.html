ABOUT THE PROTOTYPE

AquaSentinel NER

A community-first early-warning concept for water-borne disease risk.

⌁

Earlier information. Better local response.

AquaSentinel NER connects water-quality observations, community reports, health signals and seasonal/environmental information to identify potential water-borne disease risk at community level.
